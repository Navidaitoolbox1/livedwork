# LivedWork Roadmap

## Now: open skill (this repository)
- Idea Museum: four rooms, daily capture, weekly review
- Signature Interview: seven questions and a written Thinking Signature
- 30-Day Sprint and First Product: weekly plan and the Signal + Solved brief
- Works in Claude (Skill, Project, Claude Code), ChatGPT (Custom GPT, Project) and other agents

## Next: LivedWork app (planned)
- **Angle Engine**: take one common idea and turn it into several angles drawn from your own signature, influences and life
- **Creator research**: study what works for mid-sized creators in your space and extract the principles, not the ideas
- **Living museum**: capture from your phone by voice, with automatic sorting and links between ideas
- **Signature memory**: your Thinking Signature updates as you publish and learn
- **Question radar**: collect questions from comments and DMs and show which ones repeat
- **Product builder**: from a validated question to a guide, workshop or challenge, with simple hosting and email capture
- **Progress view**: pillars, consistency and real conversations over months, not just likes
