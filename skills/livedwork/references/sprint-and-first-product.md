# 30-Day Sprint and First Product

You can move faster or slower than this. The weeks are a guide, not a rule.

## Week 1: Build the museum
- Set up the Idea Museum in the tool you already use.
- Make a short list of five creators in your space with high-density ideas. Not celebrities, not unknowns.
- Capture at least one idea every day. Add at least three items to the Lived room.

## Week 2: Study, then publish
- Pick three mid-sized creators in your space. For each, find the posts that did much better than their usual. Write one line on why each one worked: the principle, not the topic.
- Take the principle, never the idea itself. Apply it to something from your own Lived room.
- Publish at least three times this week, each post anchored to one of your pillars and one real detail from your life.

## Week 3: Listen
- Keep publishing at the same rhythm.
- Track replies, saves and especially questions. Copy every question into the Questions room.
- Notice which pillar gets the most real conversation, not just likes.

## Week 4: First product
- Pick the most repeated question.
- Write a one-page guide that answers it and share it for free in exchange for an email address.
- Use the Signal + Solved rule to design a slightly bigger paid version.

Then repeat for the next twelve months.

## The Signal + Solved rule

The best first product sits where two things meet:

- **Signal:** a topic your audience has already shown it wants. Replies, questions, saves, DMs, posts that do well in your space.
- **Solved:** a problem you have personally solved in your own life or work, and can explain step by step.

Signal without Solved = you're selling something you can't really teach.
Solved without Signal = you're teaching something nobody is asking for yet.

## Small first products that work

- One-page guide or checklist
- Template pack
- A short live workshop (60 to 90 minutes)
- A 30-day challenge with a small group
- A low-priced paid community around one specific outcome
