# Signature Interview

Seven questions, asked one at a time. The goal is to make the user's point of view visible to them, in their own words.

## The seven questions

1. **The turning point.** What is one moment in your life or work that changed how you see your field? Tell me what happened, where you were, and what you did differently afterwards.

2. **The thing you learned the hard way.** What do you know now that you had to learn through a mistake, a loss or a long struggle, and that nobody taught you directly?

3. **The disagreement.** What does almost everyone in your field believe that you think is wrong, or only half true? What made you stop believing it?

4. **The people behind you.** Which two or three thinkers, teachers, books or people shaped how you think? What exactly did each one change in you?

5. **The question people bring to you.** What do people ask you for advice about, even outside your job? What do friends, colleagues or strangers keep coming back to you for?

6. **The soft skill.** What can you do with people that took you years to learn? Teaching, calming a conflict, selling without pushing, starting over in a new place, managing a team, caring for someone. Give one real example.

7. **The person one step behind you.** Describe the person you were three to five years ago. What did they want, what were they afraid of, and what would you tell them today?

## Follow-up rules

- If an answer is abstract, ask for one concrete moment: "Can you give me one example, with a place or a date?"
- If an answer sounds like common advice, ask: "Is this what you believe, or what people usually say?"
- Never ask more than one follow-up per question. Keep momentum.

## From answers to signature

- **Core beliefs** come mostly from questions 2 and 3.
- **Lived advantages** come from questions 1, 6 and 7.
- **Influences** come from question 4. Name them in the signature; credit matters.
- **Audience** is mostly the person in question 7, refined by question 5.
- **Pillars** are where a belief, a lived advantage and a repeated question overlap.
